How it works

Each operating day a remittance row opens for the branch carrying:

  • Net Sales — revenue less commission less operating expenses. This is what should be handed over, and it moves through the day.
  • Target — the baseline above.
  • Brought forward — whatever was due but not remitted on previous days.
  • Total due — target + brought forward.

Remit less than is due and the gap rolls into the next day as arrears. Beat the target and it's flagged as a surplus for that day only — surpluses never become tomorrow's credit.
